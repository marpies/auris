*Auris* is a custom, native music app for iPhone and iPad (macOS hopefully later on!) built with SwiftUI and Apple's MusicKit API. Its aim is to provide an alternative interface for exploring Apple Music and a user's personal music library, with seamless playback control from multiple devices.

> [!WARNING]
> Auris is at a very early stage of development.

## Features

- Browse songs, albums, and playlists from the user's music library
- Customize the home screen to quickly access your favorites
- Search the Apple Music catalog or personal library
- Explore genres and their charts
- View song, album, playlist, and artist details
- Revisit recent searches
- Seamless playback control from multiple devices

## Tech stack

- SwiftUI
- MusicKit
- MVVM-C
- [Swinject](https://github.com/Swinject/Swinject) for dependency injection

## Requirements

- iOS 17.0+
- Xcode with iOS 17 SDK support or newer
- An Apple Developer signing team
- Apple Music access for library and personalized content
